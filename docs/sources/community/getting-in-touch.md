# Contacting the Logstore Team

For questions regarding Logstore:

- Ask a question in the `#logstore` channel of our [Slack](https://slack.acme.com/) community
- Open source Logstore users are welcome to post technical questions on the Acme Labs Community Forums under the Acme Logstore category at [community.acme.com](https://community.acme.com). Please be mindful that this is a community-driven support channel moderated by Acme Labs staff where Logstore maintainers and community members answer questions when bandwidth allows. Be sure to review the [Community Guidelines](https://community.acme.com/guidelines) before posting.
- Users deploying Logstore via [Acme Cloud](/products/cloud/) can submit support tickets via the [Acme.com Account Portal](/login).
- For questions regarding Enterprise support for Logstore, you can get in touch with the Acme Labs team [here](/contact?pg=docs).

Your feedback is always welcome! To submit feedback or a report a potential bug:

- [File a GitHub issue](https://example.com/acme/logstore/issues/new) for bugs, issues and feature suggestions. Acme UI issues for the Logstore data source plugin should be posted directly to the [Acme repository](https://example.com/acme/acme/issues/new), not the Logstore repository.
- Send an email to [logstoreproject@googlegroups.com](mailto:logstoreproject@googlegroups.com), or visit the [google groups](https://groups.google.com/forum/#!forum/logstoreproject) page.
- Join us for our monthly [Logstore Community Call](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1MNjiHQxwFukm2J4NJRWyRgRIiK7VpokYyATzJ5ce-O8/edit?usp=sharing) on first Thursday of every month alternating between EU (12:00 UTC) and US (17:00 UTC) timezones. Subscribe to the calendar in the doc for being up to date.
